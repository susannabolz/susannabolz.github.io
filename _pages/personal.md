During Covid, I got into **Podcasts**. My favorite German podcasts are [Geschichten aus der Geschichte](https://geschichte.fm), [Feuer und Brot](https://feuerundbrot.de), [Pandemia](https://feeds.superelektrik.de/pandemia) and [Rätsel des Unbewussten](https://psy-cast.org).

I enjoy **swimming** as a recreational sport. At its best, it feels like flying, which is incredible. If you want to better understand freestyle swimming, I highly recommend the [Effortless Swimming](https://m.youtube.com/user/EffortlessSwimming) YouTube channel.  

Playing all kinds of **card and board games** is another way for me to recharge. During my time in Cologne, I learnt to play Doppelkopf, and I haven't been bored by it since. Right after Easter and Christmas, much of my free time is occupied with solving the **SZ-Rätsel** from [Oliver Rezec](https://rezec.de).
