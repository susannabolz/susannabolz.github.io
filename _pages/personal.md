During Covid I got into **Podcasts**. My favorite German podcasts are [Geschichten aus der Geschichte](https://geschichte.fm), [Feuer und Brot](https://feuerundbrot.de), [Pandemia](https://feeds.superelektrik.de/pandemia) and [Rätsel des Unbewussten](https://psy-cast.org).

I enjoy **swimming** as recreational sport. At its best, it feels like flying, and that is incredible. If you want to understand what freestyle is all about, I found the [Effortless Swimming](https://m.youtube.com/user/EffortlessSwimming) Youtube channel incredibly helpful.  

All kinds of card and board **games** are another way for me to regain energy. During my time in Cologne I learnt to play Doppelkopf and I absolutely adore the variety of house rules. Right after Easter and Christmas my free time will be occupied by solving the **SZ-Rätsel** from [Oliver Rezec](https://rezec.de).
